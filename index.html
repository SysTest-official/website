A little room to think
A quieter kind of browser

Your profiles.
Your device.

Northstar is an independent desktop browser built on Chromium and Blink. Profiles are stored on your device, so browsing data stays separated locally.

  • ✦Private tabs use a separate, non-persistent session.
  • ✦Choose your search engine and interface settings.
  • ✦Open source, made to be explored and improved.
